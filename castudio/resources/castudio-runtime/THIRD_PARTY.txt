castudio-runtime — CAStudio's notebook runtime and parametric editor.

The runtime/editor layer is adapted from SimpleCADAPI 2.2.6.post2
(https://github.com/PhySpace/SimpleCADAPI, commit
9e749ed706aa3e40cbf78b2dcfff5717737a0c2f), licensed Apache-2.0; the full
license is retained in LICENSE.  The geometry engine it was adapted from is
replaced by cadflow 0.2.0 (MIT; official Windows wheel in
resources/cadflow-wheel, native cadflow_core backend + OCCT 7.9.3).

CAStudio additions: shared-session executor, cadflow scene-package publish,
SCAPREV1 preview, project routes and the settled handshake.
